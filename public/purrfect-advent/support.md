# Purrfect Advent Support

Need a hand? Email [hello@tomatterton.com](mailto:hello@tomatterton.com) and I'll get back to you. In the app you can also go to **Settings → Help → Contact Us**, which adds your iOS and app version for you.

## Doors

**When does a door open?**
At midnight on each day from 1 to 24 December, going by the date and time zone on their iPhone. Doors for days still to come stay locked.

**What happens after Christmas?**
Every door stays open, so they can look back over the whole calendar.

**Can I add videos?**
Not yet. Doors can hold photos, GIFs, stickers and text.

## Sharing

**Do they need Pro to open it?**
No. Opening a calendar someone sent you is always free. Only sending one needs Pro.

**Can I change it after sharing?**
Yes. They'll see your changes the next time they open the app.

**How do I stop sharing or remove someone?**
Open the calendar and tap Manage sharing. From there you can remove a person or stop sharing altogether.

**The link won't open for them.**
Check they're signed in to iCloud and have the latest version of the app, then send the link again.

## iCloud

**Where is my calendar kept?**
In your own iCloud. There's no account with us, and we can't see your calendars or photos.

**iCloud isn't working.**
In the app, open Settings, then iCloud. It checks your iCloud and links straight to the fix. Apple's guide: [Sign in to iCloud](https://support.apple.com/111001).

## Pro

**What does Pro add?**
Sending calendars to someone's iPhone, and as many calendars as you like.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Go to Settings, then App Settings, tap Pro Subscription and choose Restore Purchases.

**How do I cancel Pro?**
Apple handles the subscription, so you can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings. Apple's guide: [Cancel a subscription](https://support.apple.com/118428).
